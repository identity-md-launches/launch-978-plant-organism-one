# Accounting and implementation assumptions

All value arithmetic uses base units and rounds toward backing. `floor()` is a `1e18` scaled ratio; the scale is a quote precision, not an assumption about either token's decimals. Redemption evaluates `amount * backing / remainingSupply` with full-precision `mulDiv`, then applies the live 90% payout. With nonincreasing remaining supply, a payout no greater than that proportion cannot lower the backing ratio. Once all supply has been redeemed, the last finite quote remains available as `terminalFloor`.

The supply read at binding is fixed thereafter, and is checked against PLANT on parking, redemption and settlement. The 5% threshold uses the original supply, rounded up, not the supply minus redeemed tokens. `burned` counts tokens pulled by redemption; unsolicited PLANT transfers are also irretrievable but do not change parking or the redemption denominator. There is no token rescue or allowance from this contract. Standard nonrebasing exact-transfer IMD/PLANT are required. A hostile replacement token or externally confiscated balances cannot be made safe by local accounting.

For every sip, `sip / 3` enters the gardener allocation and `sip - sip / 3` enters backing. Each cell tracks a reward-per-active-token accumulator with precision `1e27`. A pending cohort records the current day cursor. It activates only after that cursor advances; its activation accumulator is recorded after that day's rewards. Inactive cells synchronize lazily on their next use because their accumulator has not changed. Holders checkpoint on park, unpark or claim. This gives the same rewards as visiting every holder on every day, without such a loop.

To avoid stranded dust or under-reserved rewards, each cell tracks its exact aggregate outstanding scaled entitlement: an integer ceiling (`reserved`) and a fractional part below `1e27`. New accumulator increments reserve only their exact aggregate ceiling, routing the balance of the pool to backing. Checkpointing removes the holder's entire scaled entitlement, credits its integer part, and returns any newly released integer dust to backing. Fractions are forfeited toward backing when a holder checkpoints. Emptying all positions and claiming releases all gardener obligations, including allocation dust. The public `owed` equals the sum of cell reservations and checkpointed credits.

While alive, `pot + backing + owed == IMD.balanceOf(this)` after every action. Bounties, claims and redemptions have already left that balance. The same identity holds immediately at death: the `backing()` view includes all unreserved IMD and `pot()` becomes zero. `syncDeath` and redemption materialize this in storage. Death is permanent, unpark/claims remain available, and fresh deposits enter dead backing. Binding or an unbound cursor update does not restart the death clock.

Settlement visits 24 hours and at most the old and new location. All token-mutating entry points share a reentrancy guard, including bind and rotation. Hook getters at bind and token balance/supply reads are the necessary read-only external calls; subsequent value calls are only the two tokens' `transfer`/`transferFrom`. Signature recovery uses the EVM precompile. No callback, arbitrary execution, proxy, delegatecall, selfdestruct, administrator, or mutable economic parameter exists.

Validation includes the unchanged protocol digest/signature vector, happy and rejection paths, adversarial transfer mocks, full-precision redemption fuzzing, and a stateful eager per-holder reference model for rewards, custody, floor monotonicity and conservation. The latter also exits all positions to check that no debt dust is stranded. Vendored dependencies: forge-std **v1.9.7** (test helpers) and OpenZeppelin Contracts **v5.1.0** (`Math`, `SafeCast`, `Panic`, `ECDSA`), with their licenses. No compiler binary, network dependency, FFI, filesystem permission or environment configuration is needed by the tests.
